Changetrek — Walk Your Own Path

A development journey exploring life decisions and personal patterns at work. In-person sessions, weekly letters and online group reflection formed a journey inspired by Dante.

Who it was for

A development journey for people facing a life decision or exploring how personal beliefs and recurring patterns affect their daily work. The programme combined in-person sessions, independent assignments and online reflection.

The journey

Participants began with a knapsack and a silent art walk. A shared session brought the group together and helped each participant choose a personal question. Weekly letters and assignments guided them through Dante’s Inferno and up Mount Purgatory, alongside online reflection in small, facilitated groups. The journey ended with a “soul decision”: a conscious choice about their next step.

My contribution

Together with two collaborators, I developed the story, assignments and design. We provided coaching and facilitated the sessions.
